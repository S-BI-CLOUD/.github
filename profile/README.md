# S-BI CLOUD

**Secure Modular Intelligence**

S-BI CLOUD is an independent Data and Business Intelligence consulting and
engineering practice based in France.

We help organisations turn operational, financial and commercial data into
reliable decision systems — with a strong focus on delivery speed, data
integrity and controlled technical complexity.

## What we do

- Data and Business Intelligence architecture
- Decision-support and performance management systems
- Data quality, governance and process reliability
- Automated reporting and analytical workflows
- Cloud, hybrid and local deployment strategies
- Reusable analytical assets and delivery accelerators
- Applied AI and computer vision for defined business use cases

## Our approach

Every engagement starts with a business or operational question — not with a
technology.

We deliver visible, documented and reusable components designed to produce
measurable value without creating unnecessary infrastructure, maintenance or
technical debt.

Technology choices remain driven by the client’s context, security requirements
and existing environment.

## STACKBI engineering foundation

STACKBI is the internal engineering foundation used by S-BI CLOUD to standardise
delivery, document architectures and accelerate the implementation of Data and
BI solutions.

Its reusable components and proprietary mechanisms support our services but are
not publicly distributed.

## Operating principles

- Business value before tooling
- Data integrity by design
- Human validation for material decisions
- Controlled and proportionate architectures
- Cloud, hybrid or local deployment according to the actual need
- Limited dependency on individual vendors
- Documented and maintainable delivery
